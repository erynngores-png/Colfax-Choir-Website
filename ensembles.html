Ensembles

5th Grade Choir

5th Grade Choir kicks off second semester, meeting for rehearsals on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday mornings at 9:40 at Jennings Elementary. They perform at our Spring Awards Concert in May.

6th Grade Choir

6th Grade Choir meets daily at 10:05 at Jennings Elementary. This choir joins the program for the Winter Arts Showcase in December and the Spring Awards Concert in May.

Junior High Choir

This ensemble meets daily during 4th period at Colfax Jr/Sr High. In addition to our home concerts, the Junior High Choir travels to participate in regional festivals!

Concert Choir

Our high school singers meet daily during 7th period at Colfax Jr/Sr High. This ensemble caps off our home concerts, participates in regional festivals, performs in community events, and has the opportunity to compete in the annual Solo & Ensemble Contest.

Chamber Choir

New this year, Chamber Choir is a small advanced ensemble that meets before school, studying challenging music from diverse repertoire of choral traditions. Chamber Choir performs in home concerts, festivals, and community events, and travels as needed around the PNW for competition.
